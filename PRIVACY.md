# Spoolghetti Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

Spoolghetti does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal information.

## What the extension does with data

- **Reads Amazon.com search result pages** in your browser to calculate price per kilogram, filter listings, and sort them. This happens entirely on your device.
- **Loads additional pages of the same search** from amazon.com, the same way your browser would if you clicked "Next". These requests go only to amazon.com.
- **Saves your filter settings** (for example hidden brands, materials, and minimum spool size) using the browser's built-in extension storage. If browser sync is turned on, your browser syncs these settings to your other devices through your own browser account. The developer never receives them.

## What it does not do

- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry.
- No servers. The extension never sends data to the developer or any third party.
- No access to any website other than Amazon.com search pages.
- No remote code. All code ships inside the extension package.
